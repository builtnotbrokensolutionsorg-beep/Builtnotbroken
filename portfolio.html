From the Contractor

Homeowner Tips

What I'd tell my own family — how to protect your home and avoid getting ripped off.

TIP 01

Never Ignore a Crack in Your Stucco

Calgary's freeze-thaw cycle turns a hairline crack into a $4,000 problem in two or three winters. Water gets in, freezes, expands, and blows the stucco off the wall. A $300 repair today prevents a full re-coat tomorrow. If you can fit a credit card in the crack, call someone now.

TIP 02

Always Ask for WCB and Liability Proof

If a contractor is injured on your property and they don't have WCB, you can be held liable. If they damage your neighbour's fence and have no liability insurance, that's your problem too. Ask for a WCB clearance letter and insurance certificate before anyone sets foot on your property. Any legit contractor has these ready.

TIP 03

The Cheapest Quote Is Usually the Most Expensive Job

I've re-done more "cheap jobs" than I can count. A guy who charges $800 to fix your parging when the market rate is $1,500 is cutting corners somewhere — wrong mix, no primer, no moisture barrier. You'll be calling someone else in 18 months. Get three quotes, throw out the lowest, and ask the others what's included.

TIP 04

Caulking Around Windows & Doors Is Annual Maintenance

In Calgary's climate, exterior caulking around windows, doors, and penetrations typically fails every 3–5 years. When it fails, water tracks down inside your wall cavity, hits the framing, and starts rot you can't see. Walk around your house every spring and squeeze any caulk joint — if it's hard or cracked, it needs replacing. This is a $100 DIY job that prevents a $10,000 wall repair.

TIP 05

Efflorescence Is a Warning Sign, Not Just Cosmetic

That white chalky staining on your brick or concrete? That's efflorescence — mineral salts being pushed to the surface by water moving through your masonry. It means water is getting in somewhere. You can scrub it off, but if it keeps coming back, there's a water intrusion issue behind it that will eventually cause spalling and structural damage.

TIP 06

Grade Your Yard Away From the Foundation

The ground around your house should slope away from the foundation — about 6 inches of drop over the first 10 feet. If it slopes toward the house, every rain event is slowly pushing water against your foundation wall. This is the single most common cause of basement water issues in Calgary and most homeowners don't know it. A simple regrading job can save you a $30,000 waterproofing nightmare.

TIP 07

Get Everything in Writing Before Work Starts

A verbal quote is worth nothing. Before any contractor starts: get the scope of work in writing, get the price confirmed in writing, confirm the payment schedule. "50% deposit, 50% on completion" is standard and protects both sides. Never pay 100% upfront — and if someone refuses to put anything in writing, walk away.

TIP 08

Pressure Washing Stucco Can Cause More Damage

High-pressure washing can blow out mortar joints, force water behind your stucco, and damage the finish coat — especially on older sand & cement systems. If you're cleaning stucco, use a low-pressure rinse, soft brush, and appropriate cleaning solution. Leave the pressure washer for your driveway and deck, not your exterior walls.

TIP 09

Hail Damage to EIFS Is Not Always Visible

EIFS (foam-board stucco) can absorb hail impacts without showing obvious cracking — but the foam board underneath gets compressed, the mesh gets broken, and the water barrier gets compromised. After any major hail event in Calgary, get an exterior inspection even if the wall "looks fine." Insurance adjusters miss this all the time. Hail damage claims on EIFS systems are common and often covered.
