EIFS is not regular stucco, and repairs have to respect that

EIFS (Exterior Insulation and Finish System) is foam insulation board covered with a reinforced base coat and an acrylic finish. It's common on newer Calgary homes, townhouse complexes and commercial buildings. It insulates well, but the surface is thin and soft: hail, ladders, balls and woodpeckers can punch right through it.

The bigger risk is water. Older barrier-type EIFS has no drainage plane, so water that gets in at a failed sealant joint or window corner stays trapped against the sheathing. A repair that only patches the surface can hide rot that keeps spreading underneath.

We cut out damaged areas cleanly, check the sheathing behind, replace foam, embed new mesh with proper overlaps, and finish with a matched acrylic texture. We also replace failed sealant joints around windows, doors and penetrations, where most EIFS leaks start.

Signs your EIFS needs attention

  • Holes, dents or soft spots in the wall surface
  • Cracked or separated caulking around windows and doors
  • Staining or streaking below window corners
  • Bulges, blisters or finish peeling away from the foam
  • Woodpecker holes, especially on north and east walls near trees

EIFS & Dryvit Repair questions

What's the difference between EIFS and stucco?

Traditional stucco is a thick cement-based coating over lath. EIFS is foam insulation board with a thin reinforced base coat and an acrylic finish. It looks similar but repairs differently, and EIFS is softer and easier to damage.

Is Dryvit the same as EIFS?

Dryvit is a brand name for an EIFS system, and many people call any EIFS 'Dryvit.' We repair Dryvit and other EIFS brands.

Can EIFS repairs be made invisible?

We match texture and colour closely. On older, faded walls, finishing the full wall plane or corner-to-corner gives the cleanest look, and we'll tell you when that's worth it.

How do I stop woodpeckers damaging my EIFS?

Repair holes promptly (birds come back to existing ones), consider added reinforcement in repeat areas, and use visual deterrents. We can reinforce the areas that keep getting hit.

Does EIFS water damage always mean replacement?

No. Most water damage is localized around windows or penetrations and can be repaired by opening the area, replacing wet sheathing and rebuilding with better flashing and sealant details.
