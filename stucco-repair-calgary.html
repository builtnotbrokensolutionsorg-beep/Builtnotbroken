Why Calgary stucco fails, and how we repair it

Calgary is hard on stucco. We get dozens of freeze-thaw cycles every winter, chinooks that swing the temperature 20 degrees in an afternoon, hail every summer, and dry air that shrinks everything. Any water that gets in behind the finish freezes, expands, and pushes the stucco off the wall one season at a time.

That's why a cheap patch over a crack usually shows up again next spring. Most stucco damage starts somewhere else: a missing kick-out flashing at a roofline, a window with no drip cap, a deck ledger tied into the wall, or a weep screed buried under landscaping. We find where the water is coming from, fix that first, then rebuild the stucco in layers and match the texture and colour so the repair blends in.

We repair traditional three-coat cement stucco, acrylic finishes, and EIFS/Dryvit systems on houses, duplexes, townhomes and small commercial buildings across Calgary and the surrounding towns.

Signs your stucco needs repair now

  • Cracks wider than a credit card, or stair-step cracks running from window corners
  • Dark staining or streaks below windows, rooflines or light fixtures
  • Stucco that sounds hollow or feels soft when you press on it
  • Bulging or blistering in the finish coat
  • Water stains, peeling paint or a musty smell on the inside of an exterior wall
  • Stucco touching the ground or buried behind soil and landscaping (no weep screed clearance)

Stucco Repair questions

How much does stucco repair cost in Calgary?

It depends on the size of the damage, whether there's water damage behind it, and access. Small crack and hole repairs are the least expensive; section rebuilds around leaking windows cost more. We give one flat written price after seeing the job, and the estimate is free.

Can you match my existing stucco texture and colour?

Yes. We match the texture pattern by hand and colour-match the finish. On older or faded walls, the best result is sometimes to re-coat or paint the full wall plane so there's no visible patch line. We'll tell you which option makes sense.

Are cracks in stucco normal?

Fine hairline cracks from shrinkage are common and mostly cosmetic. Cracks wider than about 1/16 inch, stair-step cracks from window corners, or cracks with staining around them usually mean movement or water getting in and should be looked at.

Should I repair or replace my stucco?

Most walls can be repaired. Replacement only makes sense when damage is widespread, the sheathing behind is rotten over large areas, or the original system was installed without proper drainage. We'll show you what we find and give you both options if replacement is on the table.

Does home insurance cover stucco damage?

Sudden damage like hail or an impact is often covered; long-term water intrusion and wear usually isn't. We can document damage with photos and a written scope for your insurance claim.

When can stucco be repaired in Calgary?

Cement stucco and acrylic finishes need above-freezing temperatures to cure, so most work happens from spring through fall. Urgent winter repairs can be done with heat and hoarding, or we can make a wall watertight until spring.

Is the estimate free?

Yes, for most repairs. If a job needs moisture probing, mortar testing or opening up the wall before it can be priced honestly, we tell you the diagnostic cost up front.
